Ciao, I'm

Arman.

An Aeronautical Engineer in the making.

My MSc thesis looked at an innovative heat treatment for LPBF AA2024-RAM2 aluminium to understand how processing affects performance.

Outside the lab I work hands-on. I've inspected the performance tests of a turboblower, worked as a junior engineer at MAPNA Turbine (TUGA), and operated different machines such as CNC and manual ones in an automotive workshop. I also buy, test, refurbish if needed, and sell flame spray equipment.

Beyond engineering, I have a strong interest in business, trading, and international import and export. I enjoy exploring opportunities where technical expertise and entrepreneurship come together to make money.
